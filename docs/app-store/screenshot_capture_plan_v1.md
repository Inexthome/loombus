# Loombus Screenshot Capture Plan v2

## Goal

Prepare App Store and Google Play screenshots that present Loombus as a signal-first platform, with structured discussion as its core mechanism and Library, The Floor, Rooms, connections, Local, and Search as connected product surfaces.

## Recommended screenshot set

Capture these 8 phone screenshots first:

1. **Home / Signal Hub**
   Caption: Your signal hub
   Show the mobile Home tab and enough platform breadth to orient a new user.
2. **Structured discussions**
   Caption: Ideas become structured conversations
   Show discussion types, signal cards, and clear participation cues.
3. **Discussion detail**
   Caption: Follow evidence and what changed
   Show useful replies, evidence, and available understanding tools.
4. **Library**
   Caption: Read, highlight, and discuss
   Show a real publication, highlighting, private notes where appropriate, and the path from a passage into discussion.
5. **The Floor**
   Caption: Research investment theses
   Show research, theses, evidence, or collaborative learning. Do not imply that users can execute trades.
6. **Rooms and connections**
   Caption: Build focused communities
   Show a purposeful Room or a people-and-topic connection surface.
7. **Local and opportunities**
   Caption: Discover real-world opportunities
   Show available businesses, services, requests, jobs, events, Marketplace listings, appointments, or local opportunities.
8. **Search Everything**
   Caption: Search across Loombus
   Show multiple result types that connect the platform.

## Apple screenshot notes

Apple accepts 1 to 10 screenshots in .jpeg, .jpg, or .png.

Recommended first iPhone capture target:

- 6.9-inch iPhone portrait screenshots where possible

Useful accepted portrait dimensions include:

- 1260 x 2736
- 1290 x 2796
- 1320 x 2868

If the app supports iPad, prepare iPad screenshots too. Useful accepted 13-inch iPad portrait dimensions include 2064 x 2752 and 2048 x 2732.

Confirm current device and dimension requirements in App Store Connect before final upload.

## Google Play screenshot notes

Google Play requires at least 2 screenshots across device types and allows up to 8 screenshots per supported device type.

Recommended first Android capture target:

- 8 phone screenshots
- Portrait orientation
- At least 1080 px wide
- 9:16 aspect ratio where possible
- JPEG or 24-bit PNG with no alpha

Confirm current asset requirements in Google Play Console before final upload.

## Accuracy boundaries

Use real app screens and capabilities available in the captured build.

- Present The Floor as investment research and learning, not trade execution or personalized financial advice.
- Show an actual available publication in Library, and do not imply that every Loombus journey begins with a passage.
- Do not present AI-assisted outputs as verified facts or authoritative conclusions.
- Do not present access-controlled features as universally available.
- Show only Local and opportunity result types supported in the captured build.

Avoid:

- "Best," "#1," "Top," rankings, awards, or download claims
- Sale or discount language
- Screenshots that hide the actual interface
- Blurry, stretched, or compressed images
- Heavy marketing text that overwhelms the product
- Private data, test data, admin controls, or unavailable features

## Capture guidance

- Capture clean in-app screens first.
- Add restrained marketing frames and captions only after the raw set is approved.
- Keep one clear idea per screenshot.
- Use consistent device frames, scale, background, and caption placement.
- Make the first three screenshots explain the promise, core mechanism, and quality of participation.
- Let screenshots 4 through 8 prove the platform breadth.

## Manual capture checklist

- Use a clean account state.
- Make sure sample discussions, publications, Rooms, research, and listings look useful and safe.
- Avoid exposing private email, private messages, private notes, admin-only controls, or test data.
- Use thoughtful titles, relevant topic tags, credible evidence, and clean replies.
- Verify that every caption describes what is visibly present.
- Review small text at the final store-rendered size.
