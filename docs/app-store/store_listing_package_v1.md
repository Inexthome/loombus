## Apple App Store draft

Subtitle: Signal over noise

Alternative subtitles:

- Where ideas move forward
- Ideas into understanding
- Discuss. Learn. Act.

Keywords draft:

ideas,discussion,community,books,reading,investing,local,knowledge,debate,research

Promotional text:

Bring ideas into structured discussion, evidence, knowledge, meaningful connections, investment research, and real-world opportunities.

Description:

Loombus is a signal-first platform where ideas become structured conversations, stronger understanding, meaningful connections, and real opportunities.

Start with a question, claim, problem, or topic. Choose a discussion structure that fits the purpose, contribute evidence, and follow what changes as people respond.

Explore Loombus in several ways:

- Create and join structured discussions built for clearer context and more useful replies
- Use Library to read publications, highlight passages, keep private notes, and open a passage for discussion
- Use The Floor to research investment ideas, develop theses, examine evidence, and learn with others
- Build focused communities and private workspaces in Rooms
- Follow people and topics, make connections, and continue conversations through messages
- Discover businesses, services, requests, jobs, events, Marketplace listings, appointments, and local opportunities
- Search across Loombus and save useful discussions, publications, people, and opportunities for later
- Use available AI-assisted tools to summarize discussions, surface key points, and map areas of agreement or disagreement

Loombus is designed around a simple progression: idea to discussion to evidence to knowledge. Library offers one entry point through a passage. Other parts of Loombus begin with the question, topic, need, or opportunity itself.

The Floor supports investment research and learning, not trade execution or personalized financial advice. AI-assisted outputs can be incomplete or incorrect and should be checked against original sources. Some features may require additional access.

You deserve better than the scroll. Choose signal over noise and move ideas forward.

   Caption: Your signal hub

## Messaging guidance

Lead with: You deserve better than the scroll.

Reinforce: Signal over noise

Use this platform description:

Loombus is a signal-first platform where ideas become structured conversations, stronger understanding, meaningful connections, and real opportunities.

Use **idea to discussion to evidence to knowledge** for Loombus as a whole.

Use **passage to discussion to evidence to knowledge** only for the Library entry point.

Avoid reducing Loombus to a social media app, book-summary app, investing app, local marketplace, or single endless feed.

Avoid using **The future of social media** in the first store listing. It expresses the founder vision, but the listing should first explain the product clearly and accurately.
