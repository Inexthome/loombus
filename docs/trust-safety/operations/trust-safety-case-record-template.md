# Trust and Safety Case Record Template

Status: internal operating template
Owner: Internal Trust and Safety Lead
Tracks: Issue #667
Public ready: no

## Handling rules

- Store the completed case record only in an approved restricted system.
- Do not use public GitHub issues, ordinary chat, personal email, or personal devices as the case system.
- Do not place passwords, full payment-card numbers, unnecessary government identifiers, raw illegal sexual material, or unnecessary medical information in the record.
- Use platform identifiers and minimum-necessary personal information.
- Use role titles rather than personal names in reusable templates and public-facing outputs.
- Do not promise absolute anonymity, emergency dispatch, continuous monitoring, or a guaranteed response time.

## M. Closure checklist

- [ ] Immediate risk was addressed.
- [ ] Necessary and authorized restrictions were applied.
- [ ] Evidence references are complete.
- [ ] Evidence access history is complete.
- [ ] External referrals are documented.
- [ ] Reporter and member notice decisions are documented.
- [ ] Follow-up tasks have owners.
- [ ] No unresolved preservation request or legal hold prevents closure.
- [ ] Closure does not rely on an unsupported retention promise.
- [ ] Final status and closure timestamp are recorded.
