# Trust and Safety Operations Readiness and Legal Review Checklist

Status: internal readiness checklist
Owner: Internal Trust and Safety Lead
Tracks: Issue #667
Public ready: no

## Purpose

This checklist defines the remaining gates before Loombus can treat the Issue #667 escalation system as operationally complete or publish detailed severe-harm procedures.

Completion of documentation alone does not satisfy this checklist. Every checked item requires evidence from the applicable owner, system, controlled test, or qualified reviewer.

## A. Ownership and coverage

- [x] Primary internal Trust and Safety owner role is assigned.
- [ ] Backup Trust and Safety owner is appointed.
- [ ] Backup owner has completed access-control, confidentiality, severity, evidence-handling, and traumatic-content training.
- [ ] Consequential S1 and S2 second-review rules are operational.
- [x] Current lack of continuous after-hours coverage is documented.
- [x] Public language does not promise 24/7 monitoring, emergency dispatch, or guaranteed response times.
- [ ] Business continuity procedure is tested for primary-owner unavailability.

## B. Intake and routing

- [x] `security@loombus.com` is designated for urgent safety, abuse, threats, account compromise, and severe-harm reports.
- [x] `privacy@loombus.com` is designated for privacy concerns.
- [x] `legal@loombus.com` is reserved for formal legal and law-enforcement correspondence.
- [x] `compliance@loombus.com` is designated for regulatory and governance matters.
- [x] `support@loombus.com` is designated for routine support and S4 questions.
- [ ] Mailbox access is restricted to approved roles and verified.
- [ ] Mailbox forwarding, retention, multifactor authentication, and recovery settings are verified.
- [ ] Misrouted-report transfer is tested without exposing unnecessary information.

## C. Severity and category operations

- [x] S1 Critical definition and routing are approved internally.
- [x] S2 High definition and routing are approved internally.
- [x] S3 Elevated definition and routing are approved internally.
- [x] S4 Standard definition and routing are approved internally.
- [ ] Controlled synthetic threat scenario is completed and documented.
- [ ] Controlled synthetic child-safety scenario is completed without using real illegal material or a real child account.
- [ ] Controlled synthetic intimate-image or sextortion scenario is completed without explicit media.
- [ ] Controlled synthetic stalking or doxxing scenario is completed using fictional data.
- [ ] Controlled synthetic fraud or account-compromise scenario is completed.
- [ ] Controlled synthetic self-harm scenario verifies non-punitive help-seeking treatment and escalation boundaries.

## D. Evidence storage and access

- [x] Minimum-necessary preservation standard is documented.
- [x] Handling-log fields are defined.
- [x] Decision-log fields are defined.
- [x] Public GitHub, ordinary chat, personal email, and personal-device storage are prohibited.
- [ ] Approved restricted case-record system is selected.
- [ ] Role-based access controls are implemented.
- [ ] Evidence access is auditable.
- [ ] Authorized transfer and correction behavior is tested.
- [ ] Local-download and screenshot restrictions are enforced through training and available technical controls.
- [ ] Evidence export, backup, and vendor-copy treatment are mapped to Issue #668.

## E. Confidentiality and notice

- [x] Reporter identities are protected from reported members, Room staff, and unauthorized personnel.
- [x] Victim and witness information is restricted to documented need-to-know access.
- [x] Public language uses role titles and does not identify individual internal owners.
- [x] Absolute anonymity is not promised.
- [x] Delayed or limited notice is permitted when notice could increase danger, retaliation, evidence compromise, or legal conflict.
- [ ] Reporter receipt and outcome-notice capabilities are inventoried by module.
- [ ] Reported-member notice capabilities are inventoried by module.
- [ ] Notice templates receive legal, privacy, accessibility, and safety review.

## F. Reviewer wellness

- [x] Minimum-viewing and stop-when-sufficient rules are documented.
- [x] Unnecessary replay, enlargement, downloading, and duplication are prohibited.
- [x] Room moderators, volunteers, and untrained personnel are excluded from protected severe-harm evidence.
- [x] Review may pause when illness, fatigue, dizziness, distress, or the review environment could impair safe judgment.
- [x] Graphic details are excluded from routine notices, GitHub, and public documents.
- [ ] Backup coverage exists so a reviewer can safely pause or reassign urgent work.
- [ ] Reviewer support and post-incident debrief resources are selected.
- [ ] Training completion is documented without unnecessary medical information.

## G. Qualified legal and specialist review

The following items must be reviewed by qualified counsel or another appropriately qualified professional before Loombus publishes detailed obligations or treats the process as final:

- [ ] Child sexual exploitation reporting and preservation obligations.
- [ ] Emergency disclosure standards and approval authority.
- [ ] Law-enforcement request authentication and least-data disclosure.
- [ ] Preservation requests, legal holds, confidentiality, and delayed notice.
- [ ] Non-consensual intimate imagery, sextortion, identity verification, and representative authorization.
- [ ] Stalking, doxxing, trafficking, and victim-safety notice rules.
- [ ] Dangerous-organization taxonomy, designation use, and material-support language.
- [ ] Self-harm crisis-resource language, regional presentation, emergency escalation, and non-clinical limitations.
- [ ] Minor, teen, guardian, and Room-owner interactions.
- [ ] Evidence retention, deletion, backup expiration, and vendor-copy treatment.
- [ ] Privacy, accessibility, and reviewer-wellness implications.

## H. Dependencies

- [ ] Issue #668 establishes the canonical platform-wide retention and deletion register.

- [ ] Public severe-harm drafts are updated only after their named publication blockers are satisfied.

- [ ] Any future external provider or hotline language is jurisdictionally and operationally verified.

## I. Training and controlled verification

Support and Room moderation training must verify that staff can:

- [ ] distinguish S1 through S4;
- [ ] identify when a Room-level issue must be escalated to Loombus;
- [ ] avoid amateur investigation;
- [ ] preserve platform identifiers without copying prohibited material;
- [ ] protect reporter and victim confidentiality;
- [ ] route formal legal correspondence correctly;
- [ ] avoid promises of emergency dispatch, continuous monitoring, response time, anonymity, or guaranteed outcome;
- [ ] use the case record and decision log;
- [ ] stop or seek reassignment when traumatic exposure could impair judgment.

## J. Public publication gate

Before any detailed severe-harm policy, reporting guide, emergency procedure, child-safety procedure, or Trust and Safety help page is marked public-ready:

- [ ] All applicable legal-review items are complete.
- [ ] Backup ownership and safe reassignment are operational.
- [ ] Evidence storage is restricted and auditable.
- [ ] The retention register is approved.
- [ ] Legal-request and emergency-disclosure operations are approved.
- [ ] Controlled tests pass.
- [ ] Public text names only `Loombus Trust and Safety` or approved role titles.
- [ ] Public text contains no personal owner name.
- [ ] Public text contains no unsupported response-time, monitoring, dispatch, anonymity, appeal, retention, disclosure, or external-reporting promise.

## Completion rule

Issue #667 must remain open until every acceptance criterion is supported by operating evidence. Internal documentation may be merged before final completion, but merging it does not establish backup coverage, legal approval, restricted evidence storage, or production readiness by itself.
