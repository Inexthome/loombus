# Illegal and Regulated Goods

## Internal drafting notice

This is an internal policy draft. It requires a jurisdiction-aware prohibited-goods taxonomy, legal review, commerce operations, seller and provider enforcement, age-safety review, and module-by-module implementation verification before publication.

## Why this standard exists

Loombus supports legitimate local discovery, businesses, services, requests, jobs, events, appointments, and marketplace activity. It must not become a channel for unlawful transactions, dangerous products, exploitative services, or attempts to evade safeguards.

This standard establishes the platform-wide baseline. Marketplace, Services, Jobs, Events, Requests, Businesses, Rooms, and other modules may impose stricter rules.

## General rule

Members may not use Loombus to offer, request, buy, sell, exchange, facilitate, advertise, recruit for, or arrange goods or services that are illegal in the relevant location or prohibited by Loombus.

A transaction may be restricted even when lawful in some jurisdictions because Loombus cannot reliably verify age, licensing, location, safety, custody, or regulatory compliance.

## Prohibited categories

Subject to final legal review and a maintained taxonomy, prohibited activity should include:

- illegal drugs, controlled substances, drug-production materials, and unlawful drug paraphernalia;
- prescription medicines or regulated medical products offered outside authorized channels;
- firearms, ammunition, explosives, destructive devices, silencers, and regulated weapon parts through Marketplace or ordinary member listings;
- stolen goods, fraudulently obtained goods, or goods with removed or altered identifying information;
- counterfeit goods, forged documents, fake credentials, or unauthorized replicas represented as genuine;
- human organs, human remains, or exploitative biological material;
- sexual services, sexual exploitation, trafficking, or content involving minors;
- wildlife, endangered species, protected plants, or environmental contraband;
- hazardous chemicals, toxins, radioactive materials, or dangerous biological agents outside lawful professional channels;
- malware, stolen credentials, account access, surveillance tools intended for unlawful use, or circumvention services;
- instructions, tools, or services primarily intended to facilitate serious wrongdoing;
- unlawful gambling, unlicensed financial products, pyramid schemes, or deceptive investment offers;
- recalled, unsafe, adulterated, or unlawfully modified products;
- identity documents, government benefits, licenses, certifications, test answers, or official records offered unlawfully;
- embargoed, sanctioned, or otherwise restricted transactions where Loombus is legally prohibited from facilitating them.

## Age-restricted products

Loombus should not permit ordinary member-to-member sales of age-restricted goods unless a specific verified program exists with lawful seller eligibility, age verification, geographic controls, delivery restrictions, and compliance monitoring.

This may include alcohol, nicotine products, cannabis, gambling products, and other regulated items. Public policy must not imply that age declarations alone constitute verification.

## Weapons and dangerous items

General discussion about weapons, law, safety, history, sport, or public policy may be allowed. Commerce involving firearms, ammunition, explosives, regulated weapon parts, or weapons designed primarily to injure should not be permitted through ordinary Loombus Marketplace or listing flows.

The final taxonomy should address knives, replicas, defensive tools, fireworks, chemicals, and other items based on risk and law. Loombus should choose conservative rules where verification is not practical.

## Medical and health products

Members may discuss health products and services, but they may not:

- sell prescription-only medicine without lawful authorization;
- offer counterfeit, adulterated, expired, or recalled products;
- claim that a product cures, prevents, or treats disease without a lawful and supportable basis;
- offer human tissue, organs, or exploitative biological material;
- use misleading before-and-after images or fabricated medical endorsements;
- direct vulnerable people away from necessary care through deceptive claims.

Separate professional-claims standards apply to providers and service listings.

## Stolen, counterfeit, and infringing goods

Members must have a lawful right to offer goods. Loombus may request supporting information where a listing appears stolen, counterfeit, or unlawfully copied.

Prohibited conduct includes:

- knowingly listing stolen property;
- altering serial numbers or ownership identifiers;
- selling counterfeit branded products;
- using another seller's images or descriptions deceptively;
- selling unauthorized digital access, licenses, or accounts;
- repeated infringement after valid notice.

Loombus is not an ownership authenticator and should not promise to verify every item.

## Services and requests

The standard applies to services as well as goods. Members may not offer or request:

- violence, intimidation, stalking, surveillance, or doxxing;
- hacking, credential theft, malware deployment, or account compromise;
- document forgery or fraudulent applications;
- trafficking, sexual exploitation, or coercive labor;
- unlawful medical, legal, financial, or licensed professional work;
- evasion of taxes, safety rules, licensing, inspections, immigration law, or court orders;
- academic cheating, test fraud, or credential manipulation;
- concealment, transport, or sale of illegal goods.

## Rooms and organizations

Private Rooms may not be used to organize prohibited transactions. Room owners and moderators must respond when they know a Room is being used for unlawful or dangerous activity. Organization status, subscription level, or privacy does not create an exemption.

## Discussion versus transaction

Loombus should distinguish discussion from facilitation. Members may discuss laws, policy, harm reduction, history, news, scientific research, or personal experiences involving regulated goods. Content crosses into prohibited facilitation when it offers, requests, coordinates, instructs for wrongdoing, or directs people to a transaction.

## Reporting and evidence

Reports should identify the item or service, the relevant record, the apparent prohibited category, and any immediate safety concern. Members should avoid purchasing or handling an item solely to investigate it.

Internal review should preserve necessary listing and account evidence, avoid exposing reporter information, and use approved legal escalation where serious criminal conduct is suspected.

## Possible enforcement

Loombus may:

- reject or remove listings, posts, links, files, or messages;
- suspend commerce, messaging, Room, or account features;
- restrict or close Businesses, Rooms, or organizations;
- suspend or permanently remove accounts;
- preserve transaction, payment, listing, and audit records;
- prevent repeat or evasive listings;
- respond to lawful legal requests under approved procedures.

## Appeals

Appeals may be appropriate where an item was misclassified, offered by an authorized regulated entity, discussed rather than transacted, or permitted under a verified Loombus program. The appeals system is not yet universal, so public language must match actual controls.

## Publication blockers

Before publication, Loombus must:

1. approve a canonical prohibited and restricted taxonomy;
2. map the taxonomy to Marketplace, Services, Requests, Jobs, Events, Businesses, Rooms, and messages;
3. define jurisdiction and age-verification limitations;
4. establish seller and provider notice and appeal rules;
5. create repeat-listing and evasion review;
6. assign legal escalation ownership;
7. define evidence retention and transaction-data access;
8. complete qualified commerce and regulatory legal review.
