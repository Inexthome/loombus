# Non-consensual Intimate Imagery

## Internal drafting notice

This is an internal safety and legal draft. It must not be published until Loombus confirms expedited reporting, evidence preservation, reviewer access controls, victim-support language, synthetic-media handling, and applicable legal procedures.

## Why this standard exists

People have the right to control the distribution of intimate images and recordings of themselves. Sharing, threatening to share, fabricating, or soliciting intimate material without consent can cause severe personal, professional, financial, and physical harm.

Loombus prohibits non-consensual intimate imagery across public and private features.

## What is prohibited

Members may not post, send, upload, link to, request, trade, sell, threaten to distribute, or direct others to intimate material when the depicted person did not consent to that distribution.

This includes:

- private sexual or nude images shared without consent;
- recordings made without the depicted person's knowledge or consent in an intimate setting;
- intimate images originally shared consensually but redistributed beyond the agreed audience;
- threats to release intimate material to obtain money, sex, silence, contact, account access, or another benefit;
- altered, composited, or AI-generated intimate depictions of an identifiable person without consent;
- hidden-camera or voyeuristic material;
- links, QR codes, search instructions, usernames, file repositories, or external destinations provided to locate such material;
- requests for other members to find, identify, or distribute the material;
- posting identifying information alongside intimate material to intensify harm;
- creating Rooms, Discussions, listings, or message groups for sharing such material.

Material involving a minor is governed by the stricter Child Safety and Sexual Exploitation standard regardless of claimed consent.

## Consent requirements

Consent must be specific to the distribution at issue. Consent to create an image, send it privately, or share it with one person does not automatically authorize public posting, resale, forwarding, re-uploading, AI alteration, or use in harassment.

Consent may be invalid or unreliable when obtained through coercion, deception, intoxication, abuse of authority, threats, or exploitation.

A member who posts intimate material may be required to demonstrate a reasonable basis for believing the depicted adult consented to that specific distribution. Loombus should avoid demanding unnecessary intimate evidence from the affected person.

## Synthetic and manipulated intimate media

Loombus prohibits realistic sexualized depictions of identifiable people created or materially altered without consent, including face swaps, generative images, manipulated video, and synthetic audio used to imply sexual conduct.

Clearly fictional adult content that does not depict or identify a real person may be governed by separate sexual-content rules. It does not fall under this standard merely because it is synthetic.

## Threats, sextortion, and coercion

Threatening to release intimate material is prohibited even if the material is never posted. Review should consider:

- demands for payment or additional images;
- demands for continued contact or sexual activity;
- threats to send material to family, employers, schools, customers, followers, or Room members;
- account compromise or impersonation used to obtain material;
- repeated contact after a block or refusal;
- whether the affected person faces immediate physical danger.

These cases may also violate fraud, harassment, doxxing, child-safety, or account-security standards.

## Permitted content and exceptions

This standard does not prohibit:

- consensually shared adult content where the uploader has a reasonable basis to distribute it and the content is otherwise permitted;
- non-sexual nudity used for medical, educational, artistic, documentary, journalistic, or public-interest purposes;
- reporting or discussion of intimate-image abuse without reproducing the material;
- evidence preserved privately for lawful reporting, legal action, or safety review;
- content where the person is not identifiable and no other policy is violated.

Public-interest claims require careful review. Newsworthiness does not automatically override privacy, consent, victim safety, or applicable law.

## Reporting process

The affected person, an authorized representative, or another person with credible knowledge should be able to report:

- the content or message location;
- the depicted person's identity or relationship to the reporter, only as needed;
- whether consent was absent or withdrawn;
- whether threats, coercion, a minor, or immediate danger are involved;
- any external links or duplicate locations.

Loombus should provide a low-burden reporting route. The affected person should not be required to publicly prove identity or repost the material.

The reporting process must include a way to address impersonation and synthetic media. Identity verification, when necessary, should collect the minimum information required and use secure handling.

## Review and containment

Internal reviewers should:

- limit access to personnel who need it;
- avoid unnecessary downloads or copies;
- record the decision without reproducing explicit details;
- identify duplicate Loombus locations where technically possible;
- consider temporary access restriction while reviewing urgent reports;
- preserve evidence only under approved legal and safety rules;
- avoid notifying the reported person in a way that increases retaliation risk before containment.

Exact-byte media fingerprinting exists for certain public media, but Loombus must not claim broad perceptual matching or automatic re-upload prevention unless those systems are implemented and verified.

## Possible enforcement

Loombus may:

- remove or disable the material;
- restrict messaging, uploads, profiles, Rooms, or discovery;
- suspend or permanently remove accounts;
- close Rooms or commercial records used to distribute the material;
- preserve records for legal, safety, or appeal purposes;
- respond to valid legal requests;
- prevent ban evasion through approved identity controls.

Deliberate distribution, sextortion, repeated re-uploading, material involving minors, or organized sharing may justify immediate permanent action.

## Appeals and mistaken identification

Appeals may be appropriate where:

- the depicted person is not identifiable;
- the uploader can establish valid consent;
- the content is non-sexual or materially misclassified;
- the content is legitimate reporting, art, medicine, or documentation.

Appeal review must not require the affected person to repeatedly engage with the material or disclose unnecessary personal information. The universal appeals workflow is not yet verified, so public language must reflect actual available controls.

## Publication blockers

Before publication, Loombus must:

1. create an expedited intimate-image reporting route;
2. assign trained reviewers and escalation ownership;
3. define identity-verification and representative authorization procedures;
4. confirm evidence preservation, disclosure, and deletion rules;
5. define handling of external links and duplicate uploads;
6. align synthetic intimate media with the AI-generated media standard;
7. create victim-centered notice and safety language;
8. obtain qualified legal review.
