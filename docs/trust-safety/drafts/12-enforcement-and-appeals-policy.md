# Enforcement and Appeals Policy

## Internal drafting notice

This document defines the proposed Loombus-wide enforcement and procedural-fairness standard. It is not a description of a fully implemented universal appeals system. Public release is blocked until member notices, reason codes, enforcement history, appeal intake, eligibility, status tracking, reviewer assignment, restoration, and retention are implemented and production-tested.

## Purpose

Loombus needs an enforcement system that protects people and platform integrity without treating every mistake, disagreement, or violation the same way. Members should understand what happened, why action was taken, how long restrictions may last, and whether a decision can be reviewed.

The objectives are:

- reduce immediate and ongoing harm;
- apply standards consistently and proportionately;
- preserve context and evidence;
- provide meaningful notice where safe and lawful;
- correct errors;
- prevent retaliation, manipulation, and evasion;
- maintain records sufficient for safety, legal, audit, and transparency needs.

## Scope

This policy should apply to decisions involving:

- Discussions, Replies, profiles, messages, attachments, and accounts;
- public and private Rooms;
- Room membership, moderation, and governance actions where Loombus intervenes;
- Businesses, Services, Requests, Jobs, Events, Marketplace, Appointments, Local, and Intelligent Matching;
- Search eligibility, Featured Signal, recommendations, and AI-assisted features;
- subscription or billing-related access where the decision is based on policy rather than payment state;
- developer, automation, scraping, or platform-integrity violations.

Room owners and moderators may make Room-level decisions under their published rules. Those decisions should be distinguished from Loombus-wide enforcement.

## Decision sources

A review may begin because of:

- a member report;
- a Room moderator or owner report;
- automated or rules-based detection;
- duplicate or media-review signals;
- administrator review;
- legal notice or court order;
- payment, account-security, or fraud signals;
- credible off-platform information showing a substantial Loombus safety risk.

Automated signals should not be treated as conclusive proof where context or identity matters.

## Enforcement outcomes

Depending on severity and product capability, Loombus may use one or more of the following:

### No violation found

The content or account remains available. Loombus may still provide education, privacy guidance, or a contextual notice without recording a violation.

### Education or pre-publication warning

The member may be asked to revise content or review a rule before proceeding. A warning does not mean the content is approved, accurate, or safe.

### Content label or context notice

Content may remain available with a notice about sensitivity, disputed context, synthetic media, or another relevant limitation. This outcome may be used only where the product supports it.

### Distribution restriction

Content may be removed from Featured Signal, recommendations, Search, discovery, previews, or other amplified surfaces while remaining accessible through an authorized direct route.

### Content removal or attachment disablement

A Discussion, Reply, message, profile element, listing, file, image, video, link, or other record may be removed, hidden, or disabled.

### Feature restriction

Loombus may restrict posting, replying, messaging, following, uploading, reporting, invitations, Room management, commerce, AI use, or another feature for a defined or conditional period.

### Account warning

A warning records a policy violation and explains the relevant rule and consequence of repeated conduct.

### Temporary suspension

Account access may be limited for a specified period or until a condition is satisfied. The notice should identify what remains accessible, including billing, data, appeal, or support controls where applicable.

### Permanent account removal

Severe or repeated violations may result in permanent loss of account access. Loombus may retain limited protected identifiers to prevent ban evasion where legally permitted and disclosed.

### Room action

Loombus may restrict, suspend, remove leadership, freeze activity, or close a Room when the Room's purpose, leadership, or sustained operation violates Loombus-wide standards.

### Commercial-record action

Listings, Businesses, Jobs, Events, Services, Requests, Appointments, or related privileges may be rejected, suspended, removed, or restricted.

### Legal or emergency action

Loombus may preserve records, comply with lawful process, or make a permitted emergency disclosure under approved procedures. No public response-time promise should be made until the process is staffed and verified.

## Severity factors

Reviewers should consider:

- actual and potential harm;
- imminence and target specificity;
- involvement of minors or vulnerable people;
- threats, exploitation, intimate content, doxxing, fraud, or account compromise;
- intent, knowledge, recklessness, and efforts to conceal conduct;
- frequency, duration, coordination, reach, and monetization;
- whether a member bypassed blocks, privacy controls, prior restrictions, or Room removal;
- prior relevant violations;
- whether content documents, condemns, reports, or seeks help regarding harmful conduct;
- whether a narrower intervention can adequately reduce risk;
- legal obligations and preservation requirements.

Severe first offenses may justify permanent action. Lower-severity violations should not automatically receive the same consequence.

## Member notice standard

Where notice is safe, lawful, and operationally possible, it should include:

- the action taken;
- the affected content, feature, Room, record, or account;
- the policy category and plain-language reason;
- the effective time and duration, if applicable;
- whether the decision affects other content or privileges;
- whether review is available and how to request it;
- the deadline for review, if any;
- what information may be submitted;
- any immediate safety or billing steps.

Notices should avoid exposing reporter identities, confidential evidence, security methods, private victim information, or information that would enable evasion.

## Appeal eligibility

The target system should define which decisions are appealable. Generally eligible decisions may include:

- removal or restriction based on contextual interpretation;
- account warnings, feature restrictions, suspensions, or permanent removals;
- Room restrictions or closures;
- listing or commercial-record removals;
- mistaken age, identity, ownership, or duplicate classification;
- synthetic-media or intellectual-property classifications;
- decisions where relevant new evidence is available.

Appeals may be limited or unavailable where:

- law prohibits disclosure or review;
- a court order controls the outcome;
- the request repeats a completed review without new information;
- the requester lacks authority over the affected account or content;
- the appeal itself contains threats, exploitation, or abusive material;
- urgent restrictions must remain during review to reduce credible harm.

These limitations require legal review.

## Appeal process target

A complete system should:

1. authenticate the requester or verify authorized representation;
2. identify the exact decision being challenged;
3. accept concise context and supporting information;
4. provide a confirmation and case status;
5. assign review to a qualified person not solely responsible for the original decision where practical;
6. protect reporter and victim confidentiality;
7. evaluate the original evidence, policy, context, and new information;
8. record the outcome and reasoning;
9. notify the requester;
10. restore content or privileges where the decision changes;
11. preserve the review record under the approved retention schedule.

Loombus should avoid requiring a member to resubmit illegal or traumatic material when the platform already possesses the evidence.

## Appeal outcomes

Possible outcomes include:

- **Upheld:** the original decision remains.
- **Modified:** the violation remains, but the action, duration, scope, or reason changes.
- **Reversed:** the violation or action is removed.
- **Remanded:** additional specialist, legal, safety, or technical review is required.
- **Unable to review:** the request lacks authority, does not identify a decision, or is controlled by law or another binding process.

## Restoration

When a decision is reversed or modified, Loombus should, where technically and legally possible:

- restore content or account access;
- remove or correct associated violation history;
- restore Search, recommendation, Room, or commerce eligibility;
- correct notices or status labels;
- notify affected owners or moderators when necessary;
- document any element that cannot be restored.

Restoration does not require deleting legal holds, fraud records, security records, or valid independent violations.

## Room-level decisions

Room owners and moderators may apply Room rules. A Room-level appeal, where offered, should not prevent a member from reporting Loombus-wide safety violations. Loombus should not imply that it reviews every ordinary Room rule dispute.

Loombus-wide action against a Room or Room leader should follow this policy and include notice and review where appropriate.

## Repeated violations and evasion

Relevant prior violations may increase consequences. Loombus should distinguish:

- repeated similar misconduct;
- multiple distinct low-severity mistakes;
- severe first offenses;
- automated or coordinated abuse;
- attempts to evade account, content, Room, or feature restrictions.

Permanent-ban tombstones may prevent exact reuse of verified identifiers. Loombus must not claim IP-based household bans or invasive device fingerprinting unless such systems are lawfully implemented and disclosed.

## Quality assurance and transparency

The mature system should support:

- standardized reason codes;
- reviewer guidance and calibration;
- specialist escalation;
- conflict-of-interest handling;
- quality sampling;
- error-rate analysis;
- restoration tracking;
- aggregate transparency reporting based on reliable data;
- policy version linkage to each decision.

## Current implementation gap

Loombus currently has meaningful enforcement components across account access, Room moderation, module administration, reporting, blocking, duplicate review, and content actions. A single universal member-facing enforcement history and appeal workflow is not yet verified.

Until that gap closes, public documents must not claim:

- every removal can be appealed;
- every appeal has a fixed deadline or response time;
- every decision receives independent review;
- every reversed decision can be fully restored;
- all enforcement uses one strike system.

## Publication blockers

Before publication, Loombus must:

1. implement a platform-wide enforcement-history and notice surface;
2. create standardized reason codes and policy-version references;
3. build authenticated appeal intake and status tracking;
4. define eligibility, deadlines, reviewer independence, and repeat requests;
5. implement restoration and exception reporting;
6. establish safety, legal, privacy, support, and specialist escalation;
7. define retention and access for enforcement records;
8. verify every module named in scope;
9. complete accessibility and qualified legal review.
