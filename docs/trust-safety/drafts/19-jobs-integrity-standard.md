# Jobs Integrity Standard

## Internal drafting notice

This is an internal Jobs policy draft. It requires employer, applicant, moderation, discrimination, teen eligibility, fraud, privacy, data-retention, and legal review before publication. It must match actual Job creation, application, lifecycle, reporting, and administrator behavior.

## Purpose

Loombus Jobs is intended to help people discover legitimate employment opportunities and understand how to apply. It must not be used for fraudulent recruiting, discriminatory exclusion, exploitative labor, identity theft, trafficking, deceptive compensation, or collection of unnecessary sensitive information.

Loombus is not the employer, recruiter, background-check provider, credential verifier, payroll provider, or guarantor of a Job unless a separate written arrangement says otherwise.

## Employer and poster identity

A Job poster must have a legitimate basis to represent the employer, business, organization, client, or opportunity.

Posters may not:

- impersonate an employer or recruiter;
- falsely claim employment authority;
- use a confusingly similar business identity;
- misrepresent Loombus verification or endorsement;
- conceal a staffing, referral, franchise, commission, or agency relationship when that fact is material;
- direct applicants to a fake website, email domain, form, or messaging account;
- post Jobs on behalf of a prohibited or unavailable business.

Loombus should not describe an employer as verified unless a defined verification process supports that label.

## Accurate Job information

A Job record should accurately state, where applicable:

- role title;
- employer or business attribution;
- employment type;
- workplace type, such as on-site, hybrid, or remote;
- location;
- responsibilities;
- minimum qualifications;
- compensation information where provided or legally required;
- schedule and time commitment;
- application method;
- deadline;
- travel, licensing, physical, equipment, or background requirements;
- whether the role is commission-only, contract, volunteer, internship, franchise, or business opportunity.

Posters may not use a misleading title to conceal sales, recruiting, unpaid labor, adult activity, dangerous work, or a materially different role.

## Prohibited Job postings

Loombus should prohibit Jobs that:

- do not represent a genuine available opportunity;
- require payment merely to apply, interview, or receive ordinary employment consideration;
- involve money-mule activity, reshipping fraud, check fraud, gift-card schemes, or movement of illicit funds;
- collect passwords, authentication codes, full financial credentials, or unnecessary identity documents;
- recruit for trafficking, sexual exploitation, illegal activity, violence, malware, fraud, or prohibited goods;
- disguise a pyramid scheme, deceptive investment, unlicensed financial product, or unlawful business opportunity;
- require applicants to purchase inventory or services without clear lawful disclosure and a legitimate business basis;
- misrepresent compensation, hours, duties, location, employer, or application status;
- seek unpaid speculative work without clear consent and lawful structure;
- facilitate academic cheating, credential fraud, document forgery, or evasion of professional rules;
- involve illegal discrimination or retaliation;
- target minors for unsafe, sexualized, age-restricted, or unlawful work;
- direct applicants to malicious downloads, phishing pages, or account compromise.

## Discrimination and equal opportunity

Job postings and recruiting conduct must comply with applicable employment and anti-discrimination law.

Posters may not unlawfully exclude, prefer, discourage, or target applicants based on protected characteristics. Legitimate job requirements may be stated when they are lawful, necessary, and accurately described.

Loombus should not attempt to provide individualized legal conclusions through the policy. The final standard should identify prohibited discriminatory conduct and direct employers to comply with all applicable law.

## Compensation and fees

Posters must not:

- promise guaranteed income that lacks a reasonable basis;
- hide commission-only or performance-dependent compensation;
- present gross revenue as personal earnings;
- misrepresent salary, hourly rate, tips, equity, benefits, bonuses, expenses, or deductions;
- require payment for ordinary recruiting, interviews, background checks, equipment, or training through deceptive arrangements;
- send fake checks or overpayments and request funds to be returned or forwarded;
- require applicants to buy gift cards, cryptocurrency, or transfer money.

Legitimate licensing, equipment, or background requirements should be disclosed accurately and handled through lawful channels.

## Remote-work scams

Remote opportunities require heightened review when they involve:

- rapid offers without meaningful assessment;
- text-only interviews through unfamiliar services;
- checks for equipment purchases;
- requests to receive or reship packages;
- use of personal bank accounts to process company funds;
- inconsistent employer domains or identities;
- unusually high pay for minimal work;
- pressure to provide sensitive information immediately;
- requests to install unknown software.

Loombus should provide applicant safety guidance without claiming every scam will be detected.

## Applicant privacy

Employers and recruiters should collect only information reasonably necessary for the application stage.

They may not use Loombus to request:

- passwords or authentication codes;
- complete financial account credentials;
- unnecessary government identification before a legitimate need exists;
- medical, family, immigration, or protected information in an unlawful or irrelevant manner;
- private Loombus messages, Room history, viewer records, or account credentials;
- payment as a condition of ordinary application review.

The final privacy policy must explain what application activity Loombus stores and what information is sent to external application destinations.

## External application destinations

A Job may direct applicants to an external website or email. Loombus does not control the external service's privacy, security, accessibility, or hiring process.

Posters must not use misleading redirects or hide the real destination. Applicants should verify the employer and domain before sharing sensitive information.

The public guide should distinguish Loombus-hosted information from external applications.

## Teen applicants

The final policy must define whether and how teen members may discover, apply for, or be contacted about Jobs.

At minimum:

- age-restricted or hazardous work should not be promoted to ineligible teens;
- adults should not use Job postings to create inappropriate private contact;
- school, location, schedule, and identity information should be minimized;
- child-labor, work-permit, guardian, and hour restrictions require applicable-law compliance;
- Loombus must not claim to verify employer eligibility or guardian consent unless it does so.

Teen-specific product controls are not fully implemented.

## Lifecycle integrity

Posters should close, expire, or update Jobs when no longer available. They may not repeatedly reopen or duplicate Jobs to manipulate visibility.

Loombus duplicate systems may identify exact retries and high-confidence matching active records. This does not establish fraud or intent. Legitimate renewed Jobs should use supported lifecycle controls.

## Reviews, reports, and applicant conduct

Applicants may report misleading, discriminatory, unsafe, or fraudulent Jobs. They should not post sensitive documents publicly.

Applicants may not:

- submit fraudulent credentials;
- impersonate another person;
- automate abusive applications;
- threaten or harass employers or other applicants;
- use Job access for fraud, scraping, or commercial spam.

Good-faith criticism and reporting remain allowed.

## Administrator review

Jobs administrator tools may approve, request changes, suspend, resolve reports, or dismiss reports through the existing contracts. Review should consider employer attribution, publication requirements, record status, report context, and public safety.

Administrative approval does not guarantee the employer, job, compensation, or outcome.

## Possible enforcement

Loombus may:

- reject, suspend, close, or remove a Job;
- request corrections;
- disable links or application destinations;
- restrict employer, business, messaging, or Jobs privileges;
- suspend or permanently remove accounts;
- preserve Job, report, and audit records;
- prevent duplicate or evasive reposting;
- refer serious fraud, trafficking, or threats under approved procedures.

## Appeals

A poster may seek review where identity, authorization, discrimination, compensation, lifecycle, or fraud was misclassified. The universal appeals system is not yet verified.

## Publication blockers

Before publication, Loombus must:

1. confirm employer-attribution and publication requirements;
2. define the prohibited Job taxonomy and report reasons;
3. establish teen eligibility and age-aware contact rules;
4. define compensation and fee disclosures;
5. verify application data flows and external-link warnings;
6. create fraud, trafficking, discrimination, and security escalation;
7. establish Job record and report retention;
8. define seller or employer notice and appeal behavior;
9. complete employment, privacy, safety, accessibility, and legal review.
