# Community Standards Overview

## Internal drafting notice

This is an internal Loombus policy draft. It is not yet a public promise, a final legal instrument, or an approved enforcement manual. It must be verified against production behavior, reviewed by Trust and Safety, Product, Engineering, Privacy, Support, Accessibility, and qualified counsel before publication.

## Why these standards exist

Loombus was built for signal over noise. That requires more than removing clearly illegal content. It requires an environment where people can ask difficult questions, disagree directly, share evidence, build communities, and participate in useful real-world activity without being intimidated, exploited, deceived, or drowned out by manipulation.

These Community Standards establish the baseline rules for conduct and content across Loombus. They are intended to protect human dignity, informed participation, member control, and the integrity of Discussions, Rooms, Search Everything, Local, Businesses, Services, Requests, Jobs, Events, Marketplace, Appointments, Intelligent Matching, private messages, attachments, and related platform features.

The standards apply to all members, Room owners, Room moderators, businesses, service providers, employers, organizers, sellers, creators, administrators acting in a member capacity, and anyone using Loombus through an authorized account or public feature.

## Core principles

### 1. Human dignity

Members may criticize ideas, institutions, public conduct, products, services, policies, and decisions. They may not target people with dehumanizing abuse, credible threats, sexual exploitation, doxxing, or coordinated harassment.

### 2. Context matters

Loombus should consider context, including whether content is educational, documentary, scientific, artistic, journalistic, counterspeech, satire, or a good-faith discussion of harmful conduct. Context does not automatically excuse harm. It helps determine whether the content informs, condemns, documents, glorifies, facilitates, or targets.

### 3. Safety before reach

Content does not need to be criminal to create a serious safety risk. Loombus may remove, restrict, label, reduce distribution, or escalate material that creates a credible risk of violence, exploitation, self-harm, stalking, fraud, or other severe harm.

### 4. Authentic participation

Members should participate as themselves or through clearly disclosed organizational identities. Impersonation, coordinated manipulation, fake engagement, deceptive commercial conduct, and automated abuse undermine the platform's signal-first purpose.

### 5. Member control

Privacy settings, blocking, follower approvals, Room admission, Discussion visibility, and reporting tools are meaningful controls. No member may bypass or pressure another person to surrender those controls.

### 6. Responsible use of AI and automation

AI-assisted content, synthetic media, and automation may be used where permitted, but they must not be used to deceive, impersonate, fabricate evidence, manipulate civic participation, mass-harass people, or evade enforcement.

### 7. Room authority has limits

Room owners and moderators may establish additional rules for their Rooms. Those rules may be more restrictive than Loombus-wide standards, but they may not authorize conduct that violates these standards or remove a member's ability to report serious harm to Loombus.

## What is generally allowed

Loombus generally allows:

- strong disagreement and criticism focused on ideas, conduct, products, services, institutions, and public decisions;
- discussion of controversial political, religious, scientific, cultural, and social issues;
- first-person accounts of harmful experiences;
- documentation or condemnation of hateful, violent, exploitative, or fraudulent conduct;
- educational discussion of regulated goods, dangerous activities, self-harm, or criminal conduct that does not facilitate wrongdoing;
- satire, parody, and fictional content that is not used to deceive people about material facts;
- good-faith corrections, fact-checking, and counterspeech;
- commercial activity that complies with the applicable Loombus module rules and law;
- Room-specific rules that are clearly disclosed and consistently administered.

Allowed content may still receive a warning, label, age restriction, distribution limit, or contextual notice where the material is graphic, sensitive, potentially misleading, or unsuitable for broad recommendation.

## What is prohibited

Loombus prohibits content or conduct that materially involves:

- credible threats, incitement, targeted violence, or support for dangerous organizations;
- child sexual exploitation, grooming, or sexual solicitation of minors;
- non-consensual intimate imagery or sexual exploitation;
- targeted harassment, coordinated abuse, stalking, or doxxing;
- hateful or dehumanizing attacks against protected groups;
- instructions, encouragement, or glorification that creates a serious self-harm risk;
- fraud, scams, phishing, malware, impersonation, or deceptive identity practices;
- coordinated manipulation, fake engagement, spam, or enforcement circumvention;
- materially harmful misinformation in defined high-risk contexts;
- illegal or prohibited goods, services, transactions, or professional claims;
- infringement of intellectual-property rights after appropriate notice and review;
- unauthorized scraping, account compromise, or platform abuse;
- attempts to evade a suspension, ban, content restriction, Room restriction, or module-specific enforcement decision.

Separate standards define these categories in greater detail.

## Where the standards apply

These standards apply to:

- public and restricted Discussions and Replies;
- profiles, usernames, bios, avatars, and links;
- private messages and message attachments;
- private, public, and organization-managed Rooms;
- Room Discussions, announcements, files, events, forms, tasks, requests, and member activity;
- Marketplace listings and seller conduct;
- Business, Service, Request, Job, Event, and Appointment records;
- Search Everything and AI-assisted features;
- reports, appeals, support requests, and communications with Loombus staff;
- behavior outside Loombus when it creates a credible and substantial safety risk on Loombus.

Private spaces are not exempt from these standards. Privacy affects who may access content, not whether severe harm is permitted.

## How Loombus may respond

Depending on severity, context, history, certainty, and risk, Loombus may:

- show a pre-publication warning or request revision;
- label content or limit recommendation eligibility;
- reduce distribution or remove content from Featured Signal, Search, or discovery surfaces;
- remove content or disable an attachment;
- restrict replies, messages, uploads, commerce activity, or Room privileges;
- warn, suspend, or permanently remove an account;
- suspend, restrict, or close a Room;
- preserve records when required for safety, legal, fraud, billing, or appeal purposes;
- refer urgent matters to appropriate emergency or legal channels where permitted and operationally supported.

A report does not guarantee removal. A lack of immediate removal does not mean Loombus endorses the content. Some decisions require context, evidence, legal analysis, or additional review.

## Severity and proportionality

Loombus should not treat every violation alike. Enforcement should consider:

- whether the conduct creates imminent or severe harm;
- whether a person or protected group is targeted;
- whether the conduct is repeated, coordinated, or retaliatory;
- whether the member attempted to conceal, automate, monetize, or amplify the violation;
- whether the content documents or condemns harm rather than promotes it;
- whether the member has prior relevant violations;
- whether a less restrictive action can adequately reduce the risk;
- whether law, legal preservation, or public-interest considerations apply.

Severe violations may justify immediate permanent action without prior warnings. Lower-severity violations may receive education, warnings, reduced distribution, or feature restrictions before account suspension.

## Reporting

Members should be able to report the content, person, listing, Room activity, or message that presents the problem. Reports should include the most specific available reason and relevant context. False or retaliatory reporting may itself violate these standards when used to harass, silence, or manipulate another member.

Emergency situations should not rely only on an in-app report. Members should contact local emergency services when someone faces immediate danger. Loombus must not publish a specialized emergency-response guarantee until the operational process is fully established.

## Appeals and corrections

Loombus's long-term standard is to provide meaningful notice and an opportunity to challenge eligible enforcement decisions. A complete platform-wide appeals workflow is not yet verified. This draft must not be published as a universal appeal guarantee until the intake, eligibility, timing, reviewer, status, restoration, and recordkeeping systems are implemented and production-tested.

Where an existing feature provides review or appeal controls, Loombus should explain the available process accurately and avoid implying broader rights than the product supports.

## Relationship to other policies

These standards work with, and do not replace:

- the Terms of Service;
- Privacy Policy and data notices;
- Enforcement and Appeals Policy;
- Teen Safety Standards;
- Room Owner and Moderator Code;
- module-specific commerce standards;
- intellectual-property policies;
- subscription and billing terms;
- applicable law.

## Publication blockers

Before this overview becomes public, Loombus must:

1. confirm the final set of public standards and cross-links;
2. implement or accurately limit the appeals language;
3. assign Trust and Safety ownership and escalation responsibilities;
4. define standard enforcement reason codes and notices;
5. verify emergency and child-safety escalation procedures;
6. verify production reporting surfaces across every named module;
7. complete legal and accessibility review;
8. establish version history, effective date, and superseded-policy handling.

## Proposed public summary

Loombus supports thoughtful disagreement, structured discussion, useful communities, and legitimate real-world activity. It does not permit threats, exploitation, targeted abuse, deception, manipulation, or conduct that materially endangers people or the integrity of the platform.
