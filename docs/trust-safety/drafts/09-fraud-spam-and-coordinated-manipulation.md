# Fraud, Spam, and Coordinated Manipulation

## Internal drafting notice

This is an internal policy draft. It requires product, fraud, security, commerce, privacy, legal, and support review. The final version must match duplicate detection, account enforcement, administrator operations, billing, messaging, listing, and Search behavior.

## Why this standard exists

Loombus depends on authentic participation and reliable attribution. Fraud, spam, coordinated manipulation, fake engagement, and deceptive automation make it harder for members to find useful information, assess real opportunities, and trust the people and organizations they encounter.

This standard applies across Discussions, Replies, profiles, messages, Rooms, Businesses, Services, Requests, Jobs, Events, Marketplace, Appointments, Intelligent Matching, Search Everything, AI features, billing, and support interactions.

## Fraud and scams

Members may not use Loombus to deceive another person for money, property, credentials, access, personal information, labor, sexual content, business advantage, or other material benefit.

Prohibited conduct includes:

- fake investment, prize, grant, charity, romance, employment, rental, service, or purchase schemes;
- requesting payment under false pretenses;
- misrepresenting ownership, authority, qualifications, identity, affiliation, availability, inventory, or outcomes;
- advance-fee schemes and deceptive refund claims;
- phishing, credential theft, malicious links, malware, or account takeover attempts;
- fake invoices, payment notices, support messages, identity-verification requests, or billing alerts;
- impersonating Loombus, Stripe, Supabase, a Room owner, business, employer, service provider, government agency, journalist, or another member;
- using stolen payment methods, accounts, listings, images, or documents;
- recruiting money mules, reshipping intermediaries, or people to move illicit funds or goods;
- directing members to unsafe off-platform payment methods through deception or pressure.

## Spam and unwanted promotion

Loombus prohibits high-volume, repetitive, irrelevant, or deceptive activity that interferes with normal participation.

This includes:

- posting substantially identical content across multiple Discussions, Rooms, Replies, messages, or listings without a legitimate reason;
- sending unsolicited promotional messages at scale;
- repeatedly mentioning, following, inviting, or contacting people who have not shown interest;
- keyword stuffing, misleading tags, irrelevant links, or titles designed to capture unrelated searches;
- creating disposable accounts to amplify or repost content;
- flooding reports, support requests, applications, inquiries, or appointment systems;
- using comments or Replies primarily to redirect attention to unrelated products, services, sites, or accounts;
- hiding commercial promotion as neutral advice or independent testimony.

Reasonable cross-posting may be allowed when the content is relevant to each audience, clearly attributed, and not excessive.

## Coordinated manipulation

Members may not organize or participate in deceptive efforts to distort Loombus systems or member perception.

Prohibited conduct includes:

- coordinating fake engagement, reactions, replies, follows, saves, reports, reviews, attendance signals, or viewer activity;
- buying, selling, or exchanging engagement or account access;
- operating networks of accounts to create a false appearance of consensus, popularity, demand, trust, or opposition;
- directing groups to mass-report lawful content for retaliatory or political purposes;
- manipulating Signal scores, Featured Signal, Search ranking, recommendations, Intelligent Matching, or directory placement;
- concealing common control of accounts, businesses, listings, Rooms, or campaigns where disclosure is material;
- using compromised accounts or automated systems to influence platform metrics;
- coordinating harassment or commercial sabotage through reviews, reports, replies, or inquiries.

## Fake identities and impersonation

Pseudonyms and organizational accounts may be permitted. They must not be used to mislead people about a material identity, authority, endorsement, professional role, or relationship.

Members may not:

- impersonate another person or organization;
- create a confusingly similar account to divert messages, payments, followers, or opportunities;
- falsely claim to represent a business, employer, HOA, school, government body, Room, charity, or professional practice;
- use manipulated verification documents or misleading profile information;
- falsely claim Loombus endorsement, verification, or partnership.

Parody and commentary may be allowed when a reasonable person can understand that the account is not authentic and no other rule is violated.

## Automation and scraping

Automation may be permitted only where Loombus authorizes it and the use respects rate limits, privacy, access controls, and applicable developer terms.

Members may not use automation to:

- create or operate deceptive account networks;
- mass-message or mass-follow people;
- scrape private, restricted, or member-only data;
- bypass rate limits, paywalls, authentication, privacy settings, or blocks;
- generate abusive, fraudulent, or misleading content at scale;
- probe accounts, credentials, or security controls;
- republish Loombus data in ways that create safety or privacy risks.

## Duplicate detection and legitimate reuse

Loombus may identify exact retries and high-confidence duplicate submissions. Duplicate detection is intended to prevent accidental copies and obvious abuse. It is not a determination of plagiarism, copyright ownership, originality, or truth.

Legitimate reuse may include:

- recurring Events with different dates;
- renewed or reopened records through supported lifecycle controls;
- relevant cross-posting in distinct Rooms;
- templates used for authorized organizational workflows;
- updated listings with meaningful changes.

The system should not block legitimate activity merely because language is similar.

## Commercial disclosures

Members should clearly disclose material commercial relationships, sponsorships, referral incentives, ownership interests, and paid endorsements where applicable.

Businesses, employers, providers, sellers, and organizers must not create fake testimonials, reviews, recommendations, or independent-looking accounts to promote themselves.

## Possible enforcement

Loombus may:

- block or return duplicate submissions;
- reduce distribution or Search eligibility;
- remove content, links, messages, listings, or records;
- restrict messaging, following, posting, reporting, commercial activity, or account creation;
- suspend or permanently remove accounts;
- restrict or close Rooms, Businesses, listings, or campaigns;
- preserve evidence and payment or audit records;
- prevent ban evasion through approved identifier controls;
- refer suspected criminal conduct under approved legal procedures.

## Reporting and appeals

Reports should identify the deceptive claim, transaction, account relationship, unwanted activity, or coordinated pattern. Members should not post sensitive payment or identity information publicly when reporting.

Appeals may be appropriate for shared networks, authorized agencies, legitimate campaigns, recurring records, accessibility tools, research, or mistaken duplicate detection. The general appeals workflow is not yet fully verified.

## Publication blockers

Before publication, Loombus must:

1. define canonical fraud, spam, manipulation, and impersonation reason codes;
2. align all commerce and account modules with the standard;
3. confirm automation and scraping terms;
4. define payment, evidence, and support escalation;
5. establish coordinated-network review procedures;
6. document duplicate-detection limitations;
7. define commercial disclosure requirements;
8. complete legal, fraud, security, and privacy review.
