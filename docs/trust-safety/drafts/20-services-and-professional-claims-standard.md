# Services and Professional Claims Standard

## Internal drafting notice

This is an internal Services policy draft. It requires provider, business, appointments, requests, credential, health, legal, financial, privacy, safety, advertising, and jurisdictional legal review before publication.

## Purpose

Loombus Services helps members discover providers, describe needs, submit inquiries, connect Services to Requests, and arrange Appointments. The platform must not be used for deceptive qualifications, unsafe professional claims, unlawful practice, exploitation, guaranteed outcomes, or collection of unnecessary sensitive information.

Loombus is not the provider of a listed Service and does not guarantee licensing, credentials, insurance, identity, quality, safety, availability, price, or results unless a specific verified program expressly says otherwise.

## Provider identity and authority

A provider must have a legitimate basis to offer the Service and represent any connected business.

Providers may not:

- impersonate another professional, company, agency, charity, government office, or Loombus;
- claim licenses, certifications, degrees, insurance, memberships, clearances, or awards they do not hold;
- use another provider's work, reviews, staff, address, or portfolio deceptively;
- misrepresent ownership or authority to act for a business;
- create confusingly similar profiles to divert customers;
- falsely claim Loombus verification, approval, endorsement, or partnership.

Loombus should use terms such as “verified” only when a defined and documented process supports them.

## Accurate Service information

A Service record should accurately describe:

- the work offered;
- category and specialties;
- provider and business attribution;
- service location or remote availability;
- pricing structure or the basis for an estimate;
- duration and scheduling information;
- material limitations, exclusions, prerequisites, and customer responsibilities;
- required licensing or credentials;
- whether the Service is informational, educational, professional, or operational;
- whether an external site, contract, payment, or intake process is required.

Providers may not use vague or misleading language to conceal a materially different service, regulated activity, sales scheme, or prohibited transaction.

## Prohibited Services

Loombus should prohibit Services that involve:

- violence, intimidation, stalking, doxxing, harassment, or unlawful surveillance;
- hacking, credential theft, phishing, malware, account compromise, or security circumvention;
- document forgery, fraudulent applications, fake credentials, or evasion of lawful requirements;
- trafficking, sexual exploitation, sexual services, or grooming;
- illegal drug, weapon, hazardous-material, or contraband activity;
- money laundering, money-mule activity, pyramid schemes, deceptive investments, or unlicensed financial products;
- academic cheating, exam fraud, or false educational credentials;
- unlawful medical, legal, financial, immigration, tax, engineering, contracting, therapeutic, or other licensed professional activity;
- unsafe or unlawful child care, elder care, transportation, housing, or employment arrangements;
- removal, concealment, or destruction of evidence or records to evade law, safety review, billing, or legal holds;
- guaranteed manipulation of government, court, school, employment, credit, insurance, or platform outcomes;
- services designed to bypass Loombus privacy, blocking, account, subscription, or enforcement controls.

## Professional credentials and licensing

Providers are responsible for meeting applicable licensing, registration, supervision, insurance, scope-of-practice, and disclosure obligations.

A provider must not:

- practice beyond a lawful scope;
- use a protected title without authorization;
- imply that a license applies to a location or service where it does not;
- hide suspension, expiration, or material restrictions where disclosure is legally required;
- fabricate credential documents or verification links;
- represent general experience as a regulated credential.

Loombus should not conduct or imply universal credential verification. The public guide should encourage members to check the appropriate licensing authority independently.

## Medical and health claims

Health-related providers and sellers must not:

- guarantee diagnosis, cure, prevention, recovery, weight loss, fertility, mental-health, or other medical outcomes;
- discourage necessary emergency or professional care through deceptive claims;
- present unqualified advice as licensed care;
- sell prescription medicine or regulated medical products outside authorized channels;
- use fabricated testimonials, before-and-after images, or research claims;
- collect unnecessary medical information through public Discussions, listings, or insecure messages;
- exploit people experiencing serious illness, disability, self-harm risk, grief, or crisis.

Educational and wellness content may be allowed when accurately represented and not used to make unlawful or dangerous claims.

## Legal, immigration, tax, and financial claims

Providers must not:

- guarantee legal, immigration, tax, credit, debt, investment, insurance, or financial outcomes;
- impersonate attorneys, government officials, tax professionals, financial advisers, or licensed agents;
- solicit passwords, authentication codes, complete account credentials, or unnecessary identity documents;
- promise secret, insider, or unlawful access;
- direct members into unlicensed investments, debt schemes, or fraudulent applications;
- conceal fees, conflicts, commissions, referral compensation, or material risk;
- represent general information as individualized professional advice when they are not authorized to provide it.

## Home, construction, repair, and on-site Services

Providers should accurately disclose:

- licensing and insurance where applicable;
- who will perform the work;
- scope, materials, permits, timing, and exclusions;
- estimate versus fixed-price status;
- change-order and cancellation terms;
- access, safety, cleanup, and property responsibilities.

Providers may not use Loombus to evade permits, codes, inspections, environmental rules, or worker protections.

Members should independently evaluate providers and avoid granting unsupervised access or sharing security information without a legitimate need.

## Child care, education, care work, and vulnerable people

Services involving minors, elders, disabled people, patients, or dependent adults require heightened care.

Providers may not:

- claim background checks, clearances, guardian approval, school authorization, or professional supervision that Loombus has not verified;
- use a Service listing to create inappropriate private contact;
- request unnecessary location, schedule, medical, or family information publicly;
- engage in grooming, coercion, trafficking, abuse, or exploitative labor;
- present Loombus as a safeguarding or credentialing authority.

The final policy should define whether certain care categories are prohibited until a verified program exists.

## Pricing, estimates, and payments

Providers must accurately disclose the pricing basis and material fees. They may not:

- advertise a price they do not intend to honor;
- hide mandatory charges;
- demand unexpected gift cards, cryptocurrency, wire transfers, or overpayment refunds;
- send fake invoices or checks;
- misrepresent deposits, refunds, recurring charges, or cancellation terms;
- claim Loombus provides escrow, insurance, chargebacks, or dispute resolution when it does not.

Loombus subscription billing is separate from payments between members and providers.

## Testimonials, portfolios, and results

Providers must not fabricate or manipulate:

- reviews and testimonials;
- before-and-after results;
- completed-project images;
- customer counts;
- case outcomes;
- awards, affiliations, sponsorships, or media coverage;
- AI-generated depictions presented as actual work.

Illustrative or AI-generated media should be disclosed when a reasonable member could mistake it for an actual result, facility, staff member, customer, or project.

## Inquiries, Requests, and Appointments

Providers should use member information only to respond to the relevant inquiry, Request, or Appointment and for other disclosed lawful purposes.

They may not:

- add members to unrelated marketing lists without appropriate permission;
- share inquiry information with unrelated parties;
- pressure members to move off-platform to evade safety or reporting controls;
- collect unnecessary sensitive data before a legitimate need exists;
- harass a member who declines, cancels, reports, or leaves a negative good-faith review;
- misrepresent appointment acceptance, rescheduling, completion, or cancellation.

Loombus does not guarantee appointment performance or transaction outcomes.

## Member and requester responsibilities

Members seeking Services must not:

- request illegal, dangerous, exploitative, or deceptive work;
- misrepresent authority to approve work or access property;
- provide fraudulent payment or identity information;
- pressure providers to evade licensing, permits, taxes, safety requirements, or platform rules;
- harass providers or misuse reports;
- expect Loombus to guarantee or supervise the work.

## Reports and administrator review

Reports may address:

- false credentials or identity;
- prohibited Services;
- unsafe or unlawful claims;
- fraud or payment deception;
- privacy abuse;
- harassment or discrimination;
- appointment or inquiry misuse;
- misrepresented results, prices, location, or availability.

Services administrator tools may approve, request changes, remove, resolve reports, or dismiss reports through existing contracts. Approval does not verify provider quality, credentials, legality, or results.

## Possible enforcement

Loombus may:

- reject, suspend, archive, or remove a Service;
- request changes;
- disable links, attachments, inquiries, or Appointments;
- restrict provider, business, or messaging privileges;
- suspend or permanently remove accounts;
- preserve Service, inquiry, report, and audit records;
- prevent duplicate or evasive reposting;
- restrict connected Businesses, Requests, Rooms, or records;
- refer severe fraud, exploitation, threats, or unlawful practice under approved procedures.

## Appeals

A provider may seek review where credentials, scope, claims, category, pricing, identity, or authorization was misclassified. The universal appeals system is not yet verified.

## Publication blockers

Before publication, Loombus must:

1. approve prohibited and restricted Service categories;
2. define credential and verification language;
3. establish medical, legal, financial, care, and licensed-profession boundaries;
4. map inquiry, Request, Appointment, business, and payment data flows;
5. define sensitive-data collection and retention;
6. create fraud, exploitation, emergency, and regulator escalation;
7. verify provider notices and appeal behavior;
8. decide which high-risk categories require a verified program or are prohibited;
9. complete professional advertising, privacy, accessibility, safety, and legal review.
