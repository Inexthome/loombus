# Marketplace Prohibited Items

## Internal drafting notice

This is an internal Marketplace policy draft. It requires a maintained prohibited-item taxonomy, legal review, seller operations, report categories, listing enforcement, repeat-offender controls, age and location rules, and production verification before publication.

## Purpose

Loombus Marketplace is intended for legitimate person-to-person and business-attributed listings. It is not an escrow service, authenticity guarantee, regulated marketplace, or substitute for licensing, age verification, inspection, or professional advice.

This standard identifies items and transaction practices that should not be listed, requested, promoted, or arranged through Marketplace.

## Baseline rule

A listing is prohibited when the item, transaction, seller conduct, or delivery method:

- is illegal in the relevant location;
- violates Loombus-wide standards;
- presents a risk that Loombus cannot reasonably manage through its current verification and transaction model;
- requires licensing, age verification, custody controls, or regulatory supervision that Marketplace does not provide;
- is stolen, counterfeit, deceptive, exploitative, or materially unsafe.

Loombus may prohibit an item category even when some transactions would be lawful elsewhere.

## Prohibited item categories

### Weapons and explosives

Marketplace should prohibit:

- firearms and ammunition;
- silencers and regulated firearm components;
- explosives, destructive devices, and explosive precursors offered for harmful use;
- bombs, grenades, mines, and similar devices;
- weapons designed or marketed primarily to seriously injure;
- unlawful knives, switchblades, weaponized tools, or restricted defensive devices;
- instructions or kits intended to create prohibited weapons.

The final taxonomy must address replicas, antique weapons, sporting equipment, ordinary tools, fireworks, and lawful safety equipment conservatively and clearly.

### Drugs, medicines, and controlled products

Marketplace should prohibit:

- illegal drugs and controlled substances;
- prescription medicines;
- counterfeit, diverted, adulterated, or expired medicine;
- drug-production materials or unlawful paraphernalia;
- cannabis, nicotine, alcohol, or other age-regulated intoxicants unless a separately verified lawful program exists;
- products marketed for self-harm, dangerous intoxication, or evading drug testing;
- medical devices requiring regulated distribution where Marketplace cannot verify compliance.

Ordinary over-the-counter health products may still be removed if recalled, unsafe, adulterated, or deceptively marketed.

### Human exploitation and sexual content

Marketplace should prohibit:

- sexual services;
- trafficking or exploitative labor;
- intimate images or recordings offered without consent;
- sexual content involving minors;
- human organs, human remains, or exploitative biological material;
- content or items used to facilitate grooming or sexual coercion.

### Stolen and unlawfully obtained goods

Marketplace should prohibit:

- stolen property;
- goods obtained through fraud or account compromise;
- items with altered or removed serial numbers or ownership identifiers;
- government, workplace, school, or rental property offered without authority;
- lost property knowingly sold instead of returned through lawful channels;
- confidential or proprietary materials obtained unlawfully.

### Counterfeit and deceptive goods

Marketplace should prohibit:

- counterfeit branded items;
- forged collectibles, certificates, tickets, signatures, or authentication records;
- fake government or professional documents;
- unauthorized replicas represented as genuine;
- materially misleading images, descriptions, condition, origin, or brand attribution;
- boxes, labels, receipts, or packaging sold primarily to facilitate counterfeiting or fraud.

A seller must not use “replica,” “inspired,” or similar language to disguise an unlawful counterfeit.

### Personal data, accounts, and access

Marketplace should prohibit:

- passwords, authentication codes, account access, or recovery credentials;
- stolen databases, personal information, financial information, or identity records;
- social media, gaming, subscription, financial, or platform accounts where transfer is unauthorized;
- SIM cards, identity kits, or access devices offered for fraud;
- malware, phishing kits, spyware, or unauthorized surveillance tools;
- services to hack, bypass, unlock, or compromise accounts unlawfully.

### Hazardous and environmentally restricted items

Marketplace should prohibit or restrict:

- hazardous chemicals, toxins, radioactive materials, and dangerous biological agents;
- recalled products;
- unsafe batteries, contaminated food, or adulterated consumables;
- protected wildlife, endangered species, ivory, illegal animal products, or environmental contraband;
- pesticides or industrial materials offered outside lawful channels;
- goods whose transport or disposal would create an unmanaged safety risk.

### Financial and gambling products

Marketplace should prohibit:

- currency, payment instruments, gift cards, checks, or stored value offered through deceptive or high-risk arrangements;
- stolen or fraudulently obtained financial products;
- pyramid schemes, money-mule recruitment, or unlicensed investment offerings;
- gambling devices or access where unlawful or age-restricted;
- counterfeit currency or tools intended to produce it;
- loans, credit repair, debt relief, or financial products offered without lawful authorization.

### Government and regulated privileges

Marketplace should prohibit:

- government identification documents;
- licenses, permits, benefits, immigration documents, or official credentials;
- professional certificates or education credentials offered fraudulently;
- votes, jury service, legal outcomes, or public benefits;
- items subject to sanctions or trade restrictions where Loombus cannot lawfully facilitate the transaction.

### Animals and living things

The final taxonomy should define whether and how live animals, breeding, adoption fees, animal products, plants, seeds, and agricultural goods are permitted. At minimum, Marketplace should prohibit:

- illegal wildlife and protected species;
- animal fighting or abuse;
- deceptive rescue or adoption scams;
- animals offered in violation of welfare, licensing, transport, or age laws;
- dangerous or invasive species where prohibited.

Loombus should not allow live-animal sales until product, welfare, verification, and legal operations are deliberately approved.

## Restricted categories

Some categories may be allowed only under specific conditions, such as:

- event tickets;
- vehicles and vehicle parts;
- food and homemade consumables;
- cosmetics and health products;
- baby products;
- recalled-risk categories;
- electronics with activation locks;
- high-value collectibles;
- plants, animals, or agricultural goods;
- safety equipment;
- professional or industrial equipment.

Restrictions should address proof of ownership, condition, authenticity, expiration, recall status, shipping, and applicable law.

## Listing accuracy requirements

Sellers must accurately disclose:

- the actual item being offered;
- material condition and defects;
- price and currency;
- quantity and availability;
- location or shipping method;
- whether images show the actual item or an illustration;
- business attribution where applicable;
- known recalls, restrictions, or safety concerns;
- material fees or conditions.

Sellers may not use AI-generated images or stock media in a way that makes buyers believe the image shows the actual item.

## Transaction safety boundaries

Loombus should clearly state that:

- Marketplace does not guarantee identity, ownership, authenticity, condition, legality, delivery, or payment;
- members should not share passwords, authentication codes, full financial details, or government documents;
- unexpected payment changes, overpayments, gift-card requests, crypto demands, shipping intermediaries, and urgent pressure may indicate fraud;
- members should follow safe meeting and payment practices;
- reports do not automatically produce refunds or chargebacks.

## Seller conduct

Sellers may not:

- create duplicate listings to manipulate visibility;
- relist removed prohibited items under altered descriptions;
- conceal common account or business control;
- manipulate reviews, saves, inquiries, or reports;
- direct buyers to malicious links;
- demand unnecessary personal information;
- retaliate against reports or negative good-faith feedback;
- use Marketplace to move a prohibited transaction into private messages.

## Reporting and review

Reports should identify the listing, prohibited category, safety or fraud concern, and any relevant context. Members should not purchase a suspicious item solely to investigate it.

Reviewers should preserve the listing record and relevant account history, avoid exposing reporter information, and escalate weapons, exploitation, stolen goods, dangerous materials, or organized fraud appropriately.

## Possible enforcement

Loombus may:

- reject, suspend, or remove a listing;
- disable images, links, or seller messaging;
- restrict Marketplace or business privileges;
- suspend or permanently remove accounts;
- prevent duplicate or evasive relisting;
- preserve listing and audit records;
- restrict associated Businesses or Rooms;
- respond to lawful legal requests under approved procedures.

## Appeals

A seller may seek review where the item was misclassified, a lawful exception applies, the record was an educational Discussion rather than a listing, or ownership and compliance can be established. The universal appeals workflow is not yet verified.

## Publication blockers

Before publication, Loombus must:

1. approve the full prohibited, restricted, and conditionally allowed taxonomy;
2. map each category to report and moderation reasons;
3. define age, location, licensing, and shipping limitations;
4. establish repeat and evasive listing controls;
5. define high-risk escalation and evidence retention;
6. verify seller notice and appeal behavior;
7. create recall, counterfeit, stolen-goods, and safety procedures;
8. decide live-animal and food policy;
9. complete Marketplace, fraud, safety, privacy, and legal review.
