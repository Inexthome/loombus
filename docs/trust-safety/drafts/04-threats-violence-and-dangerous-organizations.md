# Threats, Violence, and Dangerous Organizations

## Internal drafting notice

This document is an internal policy draft. It requires a confirmed emergency-escalation process, legal review, dangerous-organization taxonomy, reviewer training, and production verification before publication.

## Why this standard exists

Loombus cannot support useful discussion or community activity when people use the platform to threaten others, coordinate violence, praise mass harm, recruit for dangerous organizations, or facilitate violent wrongdoing.

This standard protects open discussion about conflict, public safety, history, politics, crime, war, and extremism while prohibiting content that creates, celebrates, or materially supports a serious risk of violence.

## Prohibited threats

Loombus prohibits statements or conduct that communicate a credible intention, desire, plan, or encouragement to cause serious physical harm.

This includes:

- direct threats against an identifiable person, group, location, event, school, workplace, Room, or community;
- conditional threats where the condition does not meaningfully reduce the risk;
- coded or indirect threats that a reasonable reviewer can connect to a target;
- threats communicated through images, maps, schedules, weapon displays, countdowns, or location details;
- offers, requests, payments, or instructions to harm someone;
- threats against witnesses, reporters, moderators, public officials, service providers, customers, employers, or other members;
- threats intended to force a person to withdraw a report, review, application, listing, claim, or Discussion.

Vague anger is not automatically a credible threat. Review must consider target specificity, means, timing, intent, context, history, and surrounding behavior.

## Incitement and facilitation

Members may not:

- encourage others to commit violence against a person or group;
- coordinate attendance, transportation, weapons, targets, timing, or roles for violent activity;
- provide operational instructions intended to make a violent act easier;
- solicit funds, equipment, or personnel for violence;
- identify vulnerable targets or security weaknesses for the purpose of causing harm;
- celebrate an ongoing or recent attack in a way that encourages repetition.

Educational, journalistic, historical, scientific, or safety content may discuss tactics or incidents when the purpose is clearly informational and not operational assistance.

## Glorification of severe violence

Loombus may prohibit or restrict content that:

- praises a perpetrator for mass violence, terrorism, targeted killing, torture, or severe abuse;
- treats victims' suffering as desirable or deserved;
- uses attack footage, manifestos, or symbols to inspire imitation;
- celebrates suicide attacks, assassinations, massacres, or other severe violence;
- encourages people to become perpetrators.

Documentation, condemnation, memorialization, counterspeech, news reporting, and academic analysis may be allowed with context. Graphic media may still be labeled, age-restricted, or excluded from recommendation.

## Dangerous organizations

Loombus should maintain an internal, reviewable definition of dangerous organizations. Subject to legal review, this may include organizations or networks that:

- engage in or systematically promote terrorism, mass violence, organized hate violence, human trafficking, or severe criminal violence;
- recruit, fundraise, train, or provide material support for such activity;
- operate primarily to facilitate violent wrongdoing;
- are lawfully designated by relevant authorities, while allowing Loombus to assess non-designated entities based on documented conduct.

Members may not use Loombus to:

- recruit for a dangerous organization;
- praise or promote its violent mission;
- fundraise or provide material support;
- distribute operational propaganda or training;
- coordinate meetings, travel, procurement, or membership;
- create a Room, Event, Job, Service, Business, Marketplace listing, or other record to support the organization.

Neutral discussion, news reporting, research, human-rights documentation, peacebuilding, deradicalization, condemnation, and counterspeech may be allowed.

## Violent imagery and graphic content

Graphic content may be removed or restricted when it:

- depicts severe injury, death, torture, or mutilation without a legitimate contextual purpose;
- is used to threaten, shock, humiliate, or glorify violence;
- identifies victims in a way that creates additional harm;
- shows a minor being seriously harmed;
- is posted by or on behalf of a perpetrator to publicize an attack.

Where contextual value exists, Loombus may use labels, click-through warnings, age restrictions, reduced distribution, or search limits rather than removal. These controls must not be promised publicly until the actual product behavior is verified.

## Weapons and dangerous instructions

Discussion of weapons, self-defense, military history, sport, law, or safety may be allowed. Members may not use Loombus to:

- arrange unlawful weapon sales or transfers;
- instruct others to build or use weapons for violent wrongdoing;
- evade safeguards, serial-number requirements, screening, or legal restrictions;
- target people or facilities;
- combine instructions with intent to commit harm.

Marketplace and commercial restrictions may be stricter than general discussion rules.

## Public-interest and documentary exceptions

Content may be retained when it is necessary to:

- report news or warn the public;
- document human-rights abuses, war crimes, or criminal conduct;
- identify or condemn dangerous organizations;
- support academic, historical, legal, or scientific analysis;
- seek help or intervention;
- counter violent ideology.

The exception should be no broader than needed. Loombus may require context, remove unnecessary graphic details, limit distribution, or disable re-sharing.

## Emergency review

A report involving a specific target, location, means, timing, or imminent threat should receive elevated review. Reviewers should preserve relevant evidence, avoid unnecessary disclosure, and follow the approved emergency-escalation process.

Loombus must not promise continuous monitoring, guaranteed intervention, or a fixed emergency response time until staffing, intake, legal authority, and escalation procedures are established.

Members facing immediate danger should contact local emergency services. Loombus is not an emergency service.

## Possible enforcement

Loombus may:

- remove content or disable attachments;
- restrict search, recommendations, Featured Signal, messages, uploads, or Room access;
- suspend or permanently remove accounts;
- restrict or close Rooms and commercial records;
- preserve records under safety or legal procedures;
- make lawful disclosures when required or permitted;
- prevent ban evasion using approved identity controls.

Credible threats, recruitment, operational assistance, or praise intended to inspire severe violence may justify immediate permanent action.

## Appeals and restoration

Eligible decisions should support contextual review, particularly for journalism, research, counterspeech, and documentation. The platform-wide appeals process is not fully verified, so public language must not promise universal appeal rights yet.

Restoration should include removal of associated restrictions where technically possible and a clear record of why the original decision changed.

## Publication blockers

Before publication, Loombus must:

1. approve the dangerous-organization definition and review method;
2. assign emergency and law-enforcement escalation owners;
3. define preservation, disclosure, and documentation procedures;
4. confirm reviewer access controls and confidentiality;
5. implement consistent severe-threat reason codes;
6. define graphic-content labels and distribution controls actually supported by the product;
7. complete legal review for material-support, public-interest, and disclosure language.
