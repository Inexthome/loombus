# Hate and Dehumanizing Conduct

## Internal drafting notice

This is an internal draft. It requires legal, safety, product, and operational review before publication. It must be aligned with the final definitions used by reporting and moderation systems.

## Why this standard exists

Loombus supports debate about culture, religion, politics, identity, law, history, and public policy. It does not permit people to be treated as less human, inherently dangerous, or unworthy of equal participation because of protected characteristics.

The purpose of this standard is to distinguish criticism of ideas and institutions from attacks that target people based on who they are.

## Protected characteristics

For this standard, protected characteristics should include, subject to final legal review:

- race or ethnicity;
- national origin or nationality;
- religion or religious affiliation;
- caste;
- sex;
- gender;
- gender identity or expression;
- sexual orientation;
- disability or serious medical condition;
- age, where protected by law or relevant to safety;
- immigration status;
- veteran status;
- other characteristics protected by applicable law.

Loombus may also consider attacks based on perceived membership in a protected group, association with a protected group, or multiple intersecting characteristics.

## Prohibited conduct

### Dehumanization

Members may not describe protected groups as animals, vermin, disease, filth, invaders, contaminants, or inherently less human in a way that promotes contempt, exclusion, or harm.

### Claims of inherent inferiority or dangerousness

Loombus prohibits claims that a protected group is inherently:

- intellectually, morally, or biologically inferior;
- criminal, predatory, diseased, corrupt, or unfit to participate in society;
- deserving of segregation, removal, domination, or denial of basic rights;
- responsible as a group for the conduct of individual members.

Good-faith discussion of documented disparities, historical events, demographic data, public policy, or specific conduct is not automatically prohibited. The analysis must not convert evidence into a blanket claim that an entire protected group is inherently inferior or dangerous.

### Calls for exclusion, expulsion, or harm

Members may not call for:

- violence against a protected group;
- forced removal or segregation of a protected group;
- denial of housing, employment, education, services, or civil participation based solely on a protected characteristic;
- collective punishment;
- celebration of atrocities or severe harm directed at a protected group.

Political or legal advocacy concerning borders, eligibility rules, religious institutions, medical policy, sports categories, or other contested issues may be allowed when it addresses policy rather than dehumanizing people or calling for unlawful harm.

### Slurs and degrading labels

Slurs or degrading identity-based labels are prohibited when used to attack, intimidate, demean, or mobilize abuse. Context may matter when a term is:

- reclaimed by members of the targeted group;
- quoted for educational, documentary, historical, or reporting purposes;
- used to condemn hate;
- necessary to identify a specific incident.

Members should avoid reproducing slurs more broadly than needed.

### Hateful organizations and symbols

Support, recruitment, fundraising, propaganda, or operational assistance for organizations defined under the dangerous-organizations standard may be prohibited. Historical, educational, journalistic, artistic, or counterspeech use of symbols may be allowed when the context does not praise or promote the ideology.

### Targeted identity harassment

Identity-based attacks against an individual may violate both this standard and the Harassment and Bullying standard. Enforcement should consider the combined severity rather than treating each element in isolation.

## Conduct that is generally allowed

Loombus generally allows:

- criticism of a religion, ideology, political movement, government, institution, or doctrine;
- debate about public policy affecting protected groups;
- discussion of historical injustice, discrimination, extremism, and social conflict;
- testimony about discrimination or abuse;
- counterspeech that exposes or condemns hateful conduct;
- quoting offensive material where needed for analysis, reporting, or documentation;
- satire that clearly critiques prejudice rather than endorses it;
- discussion of demographic, medical, social, or criminal-justice data when presented responsibly and without dehumanizing conclusions;
- disagreement about identity-related terminology, beliefs, or policy, provided the discussion does not deny people's humanity or promote targeted harm.

Criticism may be sharp. It must remain focused on ideas, conduct, evidence, or policy rather than treating a protected group as inherently inferior or dangerous.

## Contextual review

Review should consider:

- who is speaking and who is targeted;
- whether the content is self-referential, reclaimed, quoted, or condemnatory;
- the surrounding Discussion or Room context;
- whether the content praises, facilitates, or merely documents hate;
- whether humor or satire is reasonably understandable as criticism;
- whether a protected group is identifiable even if coded language is used;
- whether the content is part of a repeated or coordinated pattern;
- whether the content creates a credible risk of discrimination or violence.

Coded language, euphemisms, images, memes, and symbols should be reviewed by meaning and context, not only by literal wording.

## Rooms and organizations

Room owners and moderators must not:

- establish rules that permit dehumanizing attacks against protected groups;
- use membership decisions to facilitate unlawful discrimination;
- organize identity-based harassment or collective punishment;
- conceal severe hate violations from Loombus;
- retaliate against members who report such violations.

Private Rooms remain subject to Loombus-wide standards. A Room may be restricted or closed when its purpose or sustained operation materially promotes hate or targeted harm.

## Possible enforcement

Depending on severity, Loombus may:

- remove or label content;
- restrict distribution, recommendations, search visibility, or Featured Signal eligibility;
- limit replies, messages, uploads, or Room participation;
- issue warnings or educational notices;
- suspend or permanently remove accounts;
- restrict, suspend, or close Rooms or commercial records;
- preserve relevant records for safety, legal, or appeal review.

Calls for violence, organized dehumanization, praise of atrocities, or recruitment for dangerous organizations may justify immediate severe action.

## Reporting and appeals

Reports should identify the protected characteristic, the targeted person or group, and the relevant context. Reviewers should avoid exposing a reporter's identity unless disclosure is necessary and permitted.

Appeal language must remain limited until the platform-wide appeals system is implemented and verified. Review should allow correction where context was misunderstood, especially for documentation, counterspeech, academic discussion, and reclaimed language.

## Publication blockers

Before publication, Loombus must:

1. approve the final protected-characteristic definition;
2. create consistent hate and dehumanization reason codes;
3. train reviewers on coded language, counterspeech, and documentary context;
4. align Room, account, Discussion, message, and commerce enforcement;
5. define severe-harm escalation;
6. confirm appeals and restoration handling;
7. obtain qualified legal review.
