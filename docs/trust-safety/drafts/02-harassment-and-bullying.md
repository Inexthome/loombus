# Harassment and Bullying

## Internal drafting notice

This draft is for internal review only. It must not be published until Loombus confirms reporting, notice, enforcement, appeal, and emergency escalation operations across Discussions, Replies, profiles, private messages, Rooms, and commerce features.

## Why this standard exists

Disagreement is essential to Loombus. Intimidation is not. Members must be able to challenge claims, criticize decisions, question institutions, and describe harmful conduct without being subjected to targeted abuse, stalking, humiliation, or coordinated pressure intended to force them out of a conversation or off the platform.

This standard protects people from targeted mistreatment while preserving room for direct criticism, satire, counterspeech, and good-faith debate.

## What this standard covers

Harassment may occur through a single severe act or a pattern of conduct. It may involve:

- Discussions and Replies;
- profile names, bios, avatars, or links;
- private messages;
- Room posts, announcements, tasks, events, files, or moderation tools;
- repeated follows, follow requests, mentions, tags, or message attempts;
- Marketplace, Job, Service, Request, Event, Business, or Appointment interactions;
- coordinated activity by multiple accounts;
- off-platform conduct that creates a credible and substantial safety risk on Loombus.

## Prohibited conduct

### Targeted degrading abuse

Loombus prohibits sustained or severe attacks directed at a person, including:

- repeated insults intended to humiliate or drive the person away;
- degrading sexual comments or unwanted sexualization;
- mocking a person's disability, medical condition, trauma, bereavement, or other serious vulnerability;
- attacking a non-public person over appearance, body, family, or personal circumstances;
- celebrating or encouraging serious harm that happened to a targeted person;
- using slurs or dehumanizing comparisons against a person, including when the conduct also falls under the Hate and Dehumanizing Conduct standard.

### Coordinated harassment

Members may not organize, solicit, reward, or direct others to target a person or group of people with abuse, reports, unwanted contact, negative reviews, replies, messages, or other disruptive activity.

This includes coded directions that make the target readily identifiable even when the target is not named directly.

### Stalking and unwanted pursuit

Loombus prohibits conduct such as:

- repeatedly contacting a person after a clear request to stop;
- using alternate accounts to bypass a block, message restriction, Room removal, or follow denial;
- monitoring and publicizing a person's movements, routine, school, workplace, home, or private relationships to intimidate them;
- threatening to continue contact across platforms or in person;
- using Loombus features to facilitate stalking or surveillance.

### Sexual harassment

Loombus prohibits:

- repeated unwanted sexual comments or requests;
- sexually explicit messages sent without consent;
- coercion for intimate images, sexual contact, or personal information;
- threats or retaliation after rejection;
- sexualized targeting of minors, which is also covered by the Child Safety and Sexual Exploitation standard.

### Harassment through platform systems

Members may not misuse Loombus controls to harass others, including:

- filing knowingly false or retaliatory reports;
- repeatedly sending follow requests after denial;
- manipulating viewer, notification, reply, mention, or Room-invitation systems to alarm or pressure someone;
- creating misleading listings or events to target a person;
- publishing another person's private information to invite contact or retaliation;
- abusing moderation authority to punish lawful criticism or personal disagreement.

## Conduct that is generally allowed

The following is generally allowed when it does not become threatening, dehumanizing, invasive, or persistently targeted:

- criticism of a person's public conduct, professional work, product, service, argument, or decision;
- disagreement expressed in strong language;
- negative reviews based on genuine experiences;
- reporting allegations in a responsible manner with relevant context;
- satire or parody that is not used to deceive or mobilize abuse;
- discussion of public figures and matters of public concern;
- one-time unwanted contact that stops after a clear boundary is set;
- counterspeech that condemns harassment without reproducing unnecessary abuse.

Public figures may be subject to broader criticism, but they remain protected from credible threats, doxxing, sexual exploitation, and severe or coordinated harassment.

## Context and severity

Review should consider:

- whether the target is a minor or otherwise vulnerable;
- whether the conduct includes threats, sexual content, private information, or offline pursuit;
- whether the member continued after a block or request to stop;
- whether multiple accounts or people were coordinated;
- the frequency, duration, and reach of the conduct;
- whether there is a legitimate public-interest, documentary, or safety purpose;
- whether the content quotes harassment to condemn, report, or seek help;
- whether the target is identifiable from context.

A single severe incident may justify strong enforcement. A pattern of lower-level conduct may also become harassment when viewed together.

## Room owner and moderator responsibilities

Room owners and moderators must not:

- use their role to threaten, shame, retaliate against, or expose members;
- permit coordinated targeting because the target is unpopular;
- require a member to surrender Loombus-wide reporting or blocking rights;
- reveal confidential report details beyond those who need them for review;
- remove a member solely for reporting a serious safety concern to Loombus.

Room rules may set higher civility standards than Loombus-wide rules, but enforcement should be clear, consistent, and not discriminatory.

## Reporting and immediate safety

Members should report the specific content, message, profile, Room activity, or account involved. Helpful context may include:

- what happened;
- whether it is repeated;
- whether a block or request to stop was bypassed;
- whether there is an offline threat;
- whether the target is a minor;
- whether private information or intimate material is involved.

When a person faces immediate danger, the appropriate response is to contact local emergency services. Loombus must not describe itself as an emergency service.

## Possible enforcement

Depending on severity and history, Loombus may:

- warn the member or require content revision;
- remove content or disable an attachment;
- restrict replies, mentions, messages, follows, invitations, or Room participation;
- reduce discovery or recommendation eligibility;
- suspend or permanently remove an account;
- restrict or close a Room when its leadership organizes or knowingly permits severe harassment;
- preserve relevant records for safety, legal, or appeal purposes.

Credible threats, stalking, sexual coercion, doxxing, or coordinated campaigns may receive stronger action without prior warnings.

## Appeals and restoration

Eligible enforcement decisions should eventually support notice and review. The platform-wide appeals system is not yet fully verified. Public language must describe only the appeal controls that actually exist when this standard is released.

When content or access is restored after review, Loombus should correct associated restrictions where technically possible and notify the affected member without exposing another person's confidential report details.

## Publication blockers

Before publication, Loombus must confirm:

1. cross-surface harassment reason codes;
2. block-bypass and repeat-contact review procedures;
3. emergency and stalking escalation ownership;
4. rules for Room moderator retaliation;
5. consistent member notices and appeal eligibility;
6. evidence retention and access controls;
7. legal review of off-platform conduct and public-figure treatment.
